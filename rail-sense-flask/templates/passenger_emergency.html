When you call

  • Stay calm and speak clearly.
  • Give your train number and your coach and seat.
  • Say where the train is, or the last station you passed.
  • Follow instructions from the operator and railway staff.

These buttons place a normal phone call from your device. RailSense does not answer emergency calls and does not share your location when you call.
